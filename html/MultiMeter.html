This tool will allow for testing, diagnosing and troubleshooting electrical circuits, components and devices. It has the capability to measure Voltage(V), 'current' Amps(A), 'resistance' (Ohms), 'capacitance' (Farads) as well as 'Continuity', 'Frequency' and 'Temperature (both AC and DC).

It has 6 Buttons:

  • OFF
    • To Power ON the meter rotate the Function Selector Switch to any measurement setting.
    • To Power OFF the meter rotate to the OFF setting.
    • By default the meter will automatically Power OFF after 30 minutes of inactivity.
  • Select: Toggles between
    • AC & DC: for Voltage and Current
    • Deg F & Deg C
    • HZ(Frequency) & %(Duty Cydle)
    • Continuity & resistance
    • Capacitance & Diode-test
    • NOTE: The default Function is printed in White
    • and the Secondary Function is printed in Orange
  • Backlighting: Press for more than 1 second to turn 'ON'/'OFF'
  • Range:
    • Default Value: AUTO - "Most appropriage Range is automatically selected."
    • Repeatedly press "Range" Button to cycle through available ranges.
    • Press "Range" Button for more than 1 second to return to "Auto ranging mode".
  • Max/Min:
    • When "Pressed", meter keeps track of "MAX" and "MIN" values as long as the meter continues to take samples.
    • The 'first' press of "MAX/MIN" button displays the Max Value.
    • The 'second' press of "MAX/MIN" button displays the Min Value.
  • Hold
    • Press "HOLD" Button to 'hold' the measurement on the display.
    • Press 'again' to release the display and return to live measuring.
